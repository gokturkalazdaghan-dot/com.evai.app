Approach

How we work

We don't collect data

Our apps don't ask you to sign up. No name, no email, no phone number. Data that was never collected can't leak.

We say when we don't know

If a price or a reading isn't known, the app says so. Producing a plausible-looking estimate pushes people to act on it.

It stays on the device

Anything that can run on the phone runs on the phone. What reaches a server is limited to what has to.
