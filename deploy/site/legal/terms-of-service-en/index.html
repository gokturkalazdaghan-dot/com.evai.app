1. What the service does

EVA AI shows electric vehicle drivers the charging stations near them, their known prices and technical details. Optionally, it reads your vehicle's charge level through an OBD-II adapter and warns you when the level drops.

2. Accuracy of data — important

Station and price information comes from third-party sources (Open Charge Map and charging network operators). This information may be:

  • Out of date,
  • Incomplete,
  • Different from the station's actual state.

The app never invents a price it does not know — unknown values are shown as "—". However, values we do show may still be wrong at source.

Range estimates are estimates only. Real range varies significantly with driving style, temperature, gradient and vehicle age. Do not plan a journey on the app's range estimate alone.

Liability: We are not responsible for losses arising from a station being closed, a price differing from what was shown, or range proving insufficient. Journey decisions are yours.

3. Driving safety

Do not operate the app while the vehicle is moving. Notifications and on-screen content can distract. Directions open in your phone's maps app; its safety warnings apply as well.
